# TownshipLens · concept preview video

`townshiplens-concept-preview.mp4`: a silent 90 s clip, 1440×900, 24 fps, 39 MB.

| Time | Story |
|---|---|
| 0–45 s | River-rise scenario: Baseline → River rises (+1, +2, +3 m) → Roads close → Reroute |
| 45–90 s | Marathon scenario: M1 Aerial overview → M2 Follow runners → M3 Traffic diversion |

**This is a visual concept, not the live app.** Every frame is labelled "VISUAL CONCEPT · ILLUSTRATIVE — not the live app".

- **Real TownshipLens data:**
  - City of Elmina buildings, roads and parks (OSM).
  - The indicative river flood areas at +1, +2 and +3 m, and the low-lying areas.
  - The 5.2 km course from the marathon engine, its closures and its detour pressure bands.
  - The counts shown: 19 places likely flooded, 9 cut off and 141.6 km of paths at +3 m (wheelchair profile); 176 of 300 sample trips rerouted, with a +283.8 m median detour.
- **Illustrative only:** the trees, lamps, people, cars and the water look. Water depth is not modelled. People and vehicles are not counts or delays.

Rendered on 23 Oct 2026 with a standalone three.js scene in software rendering. This branch holds the video only. It is not meant to be merged.
