# TownshipLens

**An AI assistant that understands your township, not just its documents.**

Team SunNiuBi · [Sime Darby Property University Hackathon 2026](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/universityhackathon) ("Township of Tomorrow") · **People** track

Residents ask how to reach a place ("Can I push a stroller from the mall car park to the playground without stairs?"). TownshipLens answers from a **verified** map of entrances, paths and amenities, highlights the route on a 3D view of the real site, says honestly what hasn't been checked, and turns every gap into a ranked task for the township team.

MVP site: **Elmina Urban Park + Elmina Lakeside Mall frontage, City of Elmina, Shah Alam.**
