# 0001. Enter the People track, focusing on inclusive access and facility discovery

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-14
- Deciders: whole team

## Context
Teams must pick exactly one track, and submitting the same idea to several tracks risks disqualification. TownshipLens helps residents find and reach facilities, especially residents with access needs. That maps directly onto four People sub-themes: accessibility & inclusion, getting around, digital living and community wellbeing.

## Decision
Submit in **People**. Lead with **inclusive access and everyday facility discovery** as the problem. Mention Planet links (shade and shelter for heat, flood-closed walkways for climate resilience) only as *extensions*.

## Options considered
- **People** (chosen): a direct fit with 4 of the 7 sub-themes; the human story is easy to tell.
- **Planet**: we could argue for climate resilience or green transport, but the core value is not environmental, so the "Strategic Fit" score would suffer.

## Consequences
- Impact metrics must be about people: time to find the right entrance, wrong-turn avoidance, confidence, staff effort.
- The pitch needs a real accessibility story and Malaysian statistics (OKU, ageing population, parents with strollers). See [accessibility-and-routing](../../02-research/accessibility-and-routing.md).
