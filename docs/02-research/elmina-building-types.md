## 1. Township and precincts

- **Scale:** About 5,000 ac for the original masterplan. Adding Elmina Business Park (about 1,500 ac) makes 6,500 ac. GDV is about RM27–30 bn, the target is 2040, and 150k residents are planned. [N]
- **Roads:** The Guthrie Corridor Expressway (GCE) runs along the **western** boundary. [N, Wikipedia] Federal Route 54 (Kepong–Kuala Selangor) is the nearest trunk road. The DASH highway connects from the south-east. Kwasa Sentral MRT is about 6.7 km east.
- **Old estate:** The land was formerly **Elmina Estate**, owned by Guthrie, now Sime Darby. [Wikipedia] The Business Park land came from Sime Darby Plantation in 2015–16, so it was **plantation land**. [N]
- **Postcodes:** Elmina West is Seksyen U15. Elmina East and Denai Alam are U16.

## 2. Building archetypes with specs

1. **2-storey intermediate link home, 20'×70'** (about 6.1×21.3 m). Elmina Green 1, Elmina Green 4 Type A1, Elmina Valley 2 Type A. Built-up 1,709–2,260 sq ft. [S/N]
2. **2-storey long or corner link home, 22'×80'** (about 6.7×24.4 m). Elmina Green 4 C2, Elmina Green 6 (with a 12 ft back garden), Elmina Valley Type B (double frontage, dual-key). Elmina Green 3 homes are "10 ft longer than usual". [S/N]
   - Elmina Valley 2 style: "brick pattern design and minimalist clean lines". [N]
3. **Superlink / cluster home, 36'×70'** (Elmina Ridge 1/2, 2-storey). Biophilic design: indoor skylight planting, **rooftop solar panels fitted as standard**, thermal roof insulation. [S]
4. **2-storey semi-D, 40'×80'.** The Eighth (Elmina West) has a ground-floor bedroom. Elmina Ridge Type S1 is a "Premium Hillside Semi-D" with side gardens. [S/N]
5. **Bungalow** (Denai Alam, Cypress Residences). [L]
6. **1-storey twin home / stacked townhouse** (Ilham Residence 3). [L]
7. **Shop office row**, 2- and 3-storey. Temu has 58 two-storey and 12 three-storey units, lots 22–24'×70–80', large windows and wide five-foot walkways. Jumpa and E-Boulevard are similar. [N]
8. **Serviced-apartment tower**, Kanopi Residences. 36 storeys, 499 units, a facilities deck on level 7, "Sky Pods". Its "afforestation" theme points to planted balconies [?]. Topped off in Aug 2026. [S/N]
9. **Neighbourhood mall**, Elmina Lakeside Mall. **Single level**, open-air, 17.2 ac site, about 200–214k sq ft NLA. It has a lakeside promenade, landscaped boulevards, drive-throughs, big-box units and 16 EV bays. Opened 22 Aug 2024. [N]
10. **School block** (education hub). SK Denai Alam opened 2025, SJK(C) Regent Elmina 2026, SMA Chong Hwa 2027, SMK Denai Alam about 2028. These are likely standard 3–4-storey JKR-style blocks [?]. [S]
11. **Mosque**, Masjid Denai Alam. The dome and minaret form is my assumption [?]. [L]
12. **Factory**: detached and semi-D industrial sheds in the Business Park. [N]
13. **Pavilion / sales gallery**: Elmina Pavilion (East), and the Elmina West gallery at No. 5 Persiaran Garcinia. [S]
14. **Park structures** in Wildlife Park: bird-watch towers, canopy walk, viewing deck, "Origami Pavilion", gateway. Also the **Elmina Rainbow Bridge** and a dragon-bridge lookout in Urban Park. [N]

**Style guess [?]** (common to Sime Darby Property landed stock from the 2016–24 era; check against photos):
- **Roof:** pitched gable or hip, in dark-grey or charcoal concrete or metal tiles. Newer phases (Ridge) are flatter and modern, with solar panels.
- **Walls:** white, off-white or light grey, with dark-grey or timber-tone accent panels.
- **Front:** a car porch with a flat slab over it.
- **Shop offices:** flat roofs or parapets with a five-foot way.

## 3. Landmarks residents recognise

- **Elmina Central Park**, 300 ac. A 5 km "green spine" made up of 6 parks:
  - Forest Park, 84 ac, next to Bukit Cerakah / Subang Forest Reserve (2,700 ac). It has the **Elmina Rainforest Knowledge Centre**, boardwalks and camp sites.
  - Arts & Cultural Park, 37 ac.
  - Urban Park, 35 ac. Fronts the mall; has a tiger playground, football field and cascading water.
  - Wildlife Park, 25 ac.
  - Sports Park, 108 ac.
  - Community Park. [N]
- **Elmina Lakeside Mall** and the **Elmina Outdoor Sculpture Museum** next to it. [N]
- **Sakura Lake Park** (21 ac) and **Infinity Lake Park** (8 ac), in Elmina Green. [S]
- **Elmina Green entrance:** a 200 m tree-lined welcome boulevard leading to a feature roundabout. [S]
- **Denai Alam Recreational & Riding Club** (Jalan Elektron U16/100); **E-Boulevard** commercial strip. [L/N]
- **Petrol stations, hospital:** none confirmed inside the township. Hospital Shah Alam and Columbia Asia are nearby. [L]

## 4. Land cover

- 3,480 ac of the 5,000 ac was said to be green space. Other sources give over 1,100 ac of green, 480 ac of local parks and 90 km of trails. [N]
- **Remaining developable land:** 3,583 ac (CIMB, 2018), so a large share was still undeveloped at that point. I found no 2025–26 figure. [N]
- **Today [?]:** expect large active construction areas in the City Centre and Elmina Ridge 2 (completion 2028). Expect former oil palm or cleared land to the north-west (Business Park). Forest reserve lies along the west and south-west edge.
