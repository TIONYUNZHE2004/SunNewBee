# Sime Darby Property and Malaysia Context

> Researched 14 Oct 2026. Every non-obvious claim has a source in the list at the bottom, numbered [S1]...; anything we could not verify is marked "(unverified)". Figures are as reported by SDP or the press, not independently audited.

## TL;DR

- **Who SDP is now.** Record FY2025 (year to 31 Dec 2025): sales RM4.2bn, PBT RM803.4m, ~RM4.2bn revenue, ~12,950 acres of landbank (est. GDV RM105bn), 27 active townships and developments [S1][S2][S3]. 1H2026 PBT rose 59.5% to RM635.6m [S4].
- **Where it is heading.** A new seven-year strategy, **SHIFT32** (from FY2026), shifts SDP from a pure developer towards a diversified real-estate group with more recurring income: industrial and logistics (36% of FY2025 sales), hyperscale data centres, malls and AUM ~RM5bn [S4][S5][S6]. A resident-facing township service layer fits the "recurring income / placemaking" story but is not the headline of SHIFT32, so we must frame it as value protection and sales differentiation, not a new business line.
- **Sustainability framework is our best alignment hook.** SDP's Sustainability Strategy and Roadmap 2030 (SSR2030, launched May/June 2025) has four pillars; one is **Community Experience**, whose first impact area is "Inclusivity & Social Equity: ensuring our developments are accessible and welcoming to all segments of society" [S7]. SDP's own 2025 annual report says developers "will increasingly need to embed age-inclusive considerations" including wheelchair-accessible walkways [S2].
- **There is a precedent we can cite.** SDP and UNICEF opened Malaysia's first inclusive playground in City of Elmina (March 2022), designed to Universal Design principles and MS 1184, with an open-source toolkit for developers and councils [S8][S9].
- **Digital footprint today is thin on the resident side.** SDP has an owner-focused "SDP App" (billing, construction progress, defects, handover), an "Elmina Valley Community" app listing, and a KLGCC app; the IAR says the SDP App grows about 3% month-on-month [S2][S10][S11]. We found no public evidence of an AI, wayfinding or accessibility feature for residents (unverified absence).
- **People to know.** Group MD and CEO: Dato' Seri Azmir Merican. Chief Digital & Technology Officer: Elmen Tan Yew Seng (listed in the 2025 annual report) [S2][S12]. The hackathon page carries messages from both but does not name them [S13].
- **Policy tailwinds.** Malaysia is an ageing society (65+ share 8.4% in 2026), Budget 2027 (tabled 9 Oct 2026) pushes an "AI nation by 2030" and RM1.5bn OKU assistance, and an AI Governance Bill is in drafting after a July 2026 consultation [S14][S15][S16][S17]. PDPA 2024 amendments (breach notification, DPO, portability) are in force since 1 Jun 2025 [S18].

### 1.3 Strategy

- **SHIFT25 to SHIFT32.** SHIFT25 (five years) ended with the record 2025. **SHIFT32** is a seven-year plan to become a "leading regional diversified real estate company", capital-efficient, with a larger recurring-income share and AUM growth through venture funds [S6]. A shareholder cited a 70:30 recurring to non-recurring balance at the June 2026 AGM; this is a shareholder remark, not confirmed company policy (unverified) [S6].
- **Industrial and logistics and data centres.** DC1 hyperscale data centre at Elmina Business Park completed March 2026; a RM2.6bn sukuk programme for build-to-suit-to-lease data centres and logistics was set up in July 2026 (Maybank IB, OCBC Al-Amin, ADB as partners; described as a green sukuk) [S4][S22]. A RM450m automated MYDIN distribution centre in City of Elmina is due 2027 [S1].
- **Implication for us.** Leadership attention is on capital markets and industrial assets. A resident-experience pilot is cheap, safe and complementary: it supports the residential and retail sales that still deliver most earnings and matches SSR2030's "Community Experience" pillar.

## 2. Sustainability and ESG

- **Net zero.** Pledge announced 27 Nov 2023: Net Zero by 2050, 1.5 degC science-based framing, 40% Scope 1 and 2 reduction by 2030. The announcement states no baseline year and Scope 3 is still being studied [S23].
- **SSR2030** (launched mid-2025): four pillars: Energy and Carbon (-40% Scope 1+2), Urban Biodiversity (net biodiversity positive by 2030), Climate Resilience, Community Experience [S7][S24].
- **Community Experience pillar** has four impact areas: Inclusivity and Social Equity, Resilient Infrastructure (shared spaces from community parks to urban farms), Education and Future-Ready Skills, Support for the Underserved [S24]. 2025: RM21.4m sponsorships and donations, 17,800+ individuals engaged across 35 community and sustainability initiatives [S24].
- **Biodiversity flagships.** City of Elmina adjoins Bukit Cerakah Forest Reserve; SDP is building a 5 km urban biodiversity corridor; the Elmina Rainforest Knowledge Centre (ERKC) won a Silver at The Star ESG Positive Impact Awards 2025; 191,827 trees planted to date (target 250,000) [S2][S25].
- **Reports we should cite in the pitch:** Sustainability Report 2025 and Integrated Annual Report 2025 (see `reference/notes/context-sources.md`).

## 4. Universal design and accessibility at SDP

- **UNICEF Inclusive Playground, City of Elmina** (launched 2 Mar 2022; construction began Feb 2020): ramps, spinners, swings, sensory poles, balancing logs; co-designed with children with and without disabilities; Shah Alam City Council approved it as applying Universal Design and MS 1184. SDP and UNICEF released an open-source Inclusive Playground Toolkit [S8][S9]. Which park inside Elmina hosts it is not confirmed in the sources we found; an SDP brochure lists an "Inclusive Playground" under a "Community Park" zone (unverified mapping to the built layout) [S26].
- **Residential:** The Nine, Elmina Green has "wheelchair-friendly ground-floor suites"; Casira 3 at Bandar Bukit Raja has wheelchair-accessible ground floors [S2][S21].
- **Corporate narrative:** the 2025 IAR says all new residential should include wheelchair-accessible walkways and mobility-friendly access to lifts and car parks, and that placemaking must support multigenerational living [S2].
- **Legal baseline:** UBBL 34A makes MS 1183/MS 1184 apply to new buildings; MS 1184:2014 is the universal-design standard. Critics note no mandatory access audits and limited enforcement; we found no 2025/2026 amendment to the Persons with Disabilities Act 2008 on built-environment access (Oct 2025: minister said Act 685 should be reviewed) [S27].

**Pitch angle:** SDP has already invested in *building* accessibly. TownshipLens makes that investment *discoverable and verifiable* by residents.

## 5. Community facilities relevant to the MVP

- **Elmina Central Park**: 300 acres, five precincts (Forest, Arts and Cultural, Urban, Community, Sports); arboretum, amphitheatre, boardwalks, 90 km cycling/jogging network planned (25 km built at an older report date, unverified now) [S20][S28].
- **Elmina Lakeside Mall**: SDP-owned, soft-opened 30 Aug 2024, ~214,000 sq ft NLA across nine single/double-storey blocks, 180,000 visitors in opening week, direct connectivity to the adjoining Elmina Urban Park (reported ~35 acres, unverified) [S29][S30].
- **Elmina Rainforest Knowledge Centre** (3.1712 N, 101.5007 E per OSM) inside Central Park, with 13,000+ people engaged in nature programmes [S25].
- **Bandar Bukit Raja Wetland Townpark**: 180 acres, 75 km track, pavilion and lawn, 64 bird species; accessibility not documented [S21].

## 6. Malaysia policy and demographic context

| Area | Key facts | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Ageing** | Population 34.4m (2026); 65+ share 8.4% (8.0% in 2025); median age 31.7; 12 states are "ageing" (>7%); Malaysia is at "ageing society" stage; aged-society (14%) projected around 2048 (one report) | [S14] |
| **OKU** | 756,681 registered with JKM at 31 Jan 2024 (Selangor highest: 128,401; learning disability 296,782; physical 253,510); a conflicting 582,944 figure appears elsewhere (do not use without checking Hansard). Registered numbers undercount real prevalence (our note) | [S31] |
| **Budget 2027** (tabled 9 Oct 2026) | At least RM3.24bn for seniors, OKU and Orang Asli; OKU assistance RM1.5bn (>300,000 recipients); bedridden aid RM500 to RM600; Digital Ministry: 200,000 AI-skilled people, 4,000 MSMEs adopting AI. No smart-city allocation found (single news source, verify against official documents) | [S15][S32] |
| **AI policy** | National AI Action Plan 2026-2030 (targets +1.2 pp GDP, top-10 Global AI Index, 300,000 AI jobs by 2030); AI Governance Bill consultation 10-31 Jul 2026, risk-based, principles: human dignity, transparency and explainability, accountability, safety, responsible data governance; Parliament tabling forecast Nov-Dec 2026 (law-firm forecast) | [S16][S17] |
| **AIGE 2024** | National Guidelines on AI Governance and Ethics (MOSTI, Sep 2024), voluntary; seven principles incl. fairness, reliability/safety/control, privacy/security, inclusiveness, transparency, human benefit | [S33] |
| **PDPA 2024** | Amendments in force in stages: 1 Apr 2025 (processor duties, cross-border, higher penalties up to RM1m / 3 yrs), 1 Jun 2025 (DPO, breach notification, portability). Guidelines issued after 2024 consultations (details unverified) | [S18] |
| **MyDIGITAL** | Malaysia Digital Economy Blueprint (Feb 2021), 2021-2030, 22.6% GDP target | [S34] |
| **Smart city** | KPKT Smart City Framework (2018/19); 2023 minister called smart city a strategic initiative. We found no Smart Selangor 2025/26 blueprint (unverified); PLANMalaysia Selangor is preparing a *Sustainable* Selangor Planning Blueprint | [S35][S36] |

**Design implications (our reading):**
1. We hold no biometric or health data; do not ask for disability status. Log questions without identities to stay light on PDPA.
2. Show uncertainty and sources in every answer to match AIGE transparency and the draft bill's transparency/explainability principle.
3. A facility-access assistant that informs but never certifies is likely low-risk under a risk-based regime (our judgement, not legal advice).
